# ClockHell

Clockhell is a 2D Metroidvania game in the steampunk style where the main objective is to kill robots and mutants created and controlled by something greater.

## History

The year is 1846, in a universe where technology is advancing faster than usual. The initial creation of robots and weapons was a milestone in human history, but not all is well on this earth. The more intelligent and agile these machines became, the more dependent on technology people became, and slowly what was a democracy turned into a dictatorship, and it seemed that humanity would be dependent on the tyrannical government of this city. However, a mysterious man emerges, the famous "Steel Skinner," as a form of protest not only for himself but for the impoverished people of "New London." He destroys all kinds of technology used by these rich fools. His name is Nico Wellheim, son of a renowned family, but Nico wanted to follow a different path. In this story, use Nico's weapon skills and combat abilities to find the mastermind behind it all.
